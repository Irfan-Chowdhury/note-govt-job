### 🎯 Key Examination Insights & Preparation Highlights

1. **High-Frequency Mathematical / Numerical Problems:**
   - **CPU Scheduling Calculations (Topic 05, Q26–Q33):** Heavily tested in BPSC (2025, 2026), BCIC (2025), and Combined Bank Officer (2026). Questions require drawing Gantt charts and computing Average Waiting Time (WT) and Turnaround Time (TAT) for FCFS, SJF, and Round Robin (RR with time quantum $q$).
   - **Banker's Algorithm (Topic 06, Q34–Q41):** High-mark written questions (Bangladesh Bank AD ICT 2025). Requires deriving the **Need Matrix** ($Need = Max - Allocation$) and proving safety using the Work/Finish vectors to yield a valid **Safe Sequence**.
   - **Paging & Virtual Memory Address Translation (Topic 07, Q42–Q50):** Frequently asked across Combined Banks and BPSC. Requires calculating logical address bits, physical address bits, number of pages ($2^{\text{address bits}} / \text{page size}$), Page Table size ($(\text{Number of Pages}) \times \text{PTE size}$), and TLB memory requirements.
   - **RAID Storage Calculations (Topic 08, Q51–Q55):** High recurring trend in 2026 exams (Officer, Senior Officer, Assistant Engineer). Crucial formulas: RAID 0 ($N \times S$), RAID 5 ($(N-1) \times S$), RAID 6 ($(N-2) \times S$), and RAID 10 ($(N/2) \times S$).
   - **Page Replacement (Topic 07, Q48, Q50):** Tracking page frames and counting page faults using Least Recently Used (LRU) and FIFO algorithms.

2. **Most Common Descriptive / Concept Questions:**
   - **Deadlock 4 Conditions (Q35, Q36, Q37, Q39, Q40, Q41):** Appeared in 6 separate examinations. Questions test Mutual Exclusion, Hold and Wait, No Preemption, Circular Wait, and whether a single process can deadlock.
   - **Process vs. Thread (Q21, Q25):** Differentiated based on memory address space, resource sharing, PCB vs. TCB, and context switching overhead.
   - **Virtualization & Hypervisors (Q56, Q59, Q60):** Architecture diagrams of Type 1 (Bare-metal) vs. Type 2 (Hosted) hypervisors, and architectural differences between Virtual Machines (VMs) and Containers.
   - **Thrashing (Q43):** Mechanism of page-fault frequency collapse, thrashing causes in demand paging, and CPU utilization drop.
   - **Paging vs. Segmentation (Q45):** Fixed-size vs. variable-size logical division, internal vs. external fragmentation, and hardware mapping mechanisms.
